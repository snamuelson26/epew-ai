| Contribution | Up-to participation benefit |
|---|---|
| $500–$5,199.99 | 6% |
| $5,200–$20,000 | 8% |
| $25,000–$50,000 | 10% |
| $100,000+ | 12% |

The ranges $20,000.01–$24,999.99 and $50,000.01–$99,999.99 are undefined and rejected, never interpolated. No maximum above $100,000 was supplied. No extra contributions are combined or repriced into another tier without an approved aggregation rule.

Previews are watermarked by default. Typed Williams Koor identification is not an electronic signature; add only his authorized signature/seal assets. Do not forge signatures or invent received/maturity dates.
